Direction 01

The Flagship

Navy sidebar · White content · Crimson accent. Full, direct EBB inheritance. The sidebar is navy — the same navy as EBB's logo and cover pages. Every panel has a crimson left-accent border. Headings use Libre Baskerville. This reads as: this tool was built by the same people who built EBB. The most authoritative option.

Direction 04

Midnight Navy

Crimson glows as the precision accent. Feels like a Bloomberg terminal, a Reuters dashboard, a serious financial data tool — built for professionals who work late. Dark authority, no gimmicks.

Direction 08

The Consul

Navy sidebar · White content · Forest Green accent. Keeps EBB's navy authority but replaces crimson with deep forest green `#2E7D32`. Deployment = systems running = green. Infrastructure tools often use green (Terraform, Ansible, many ops dashboards). This is the most "technically appropriate" accent choice — green means go, systems live, deployment succeeded. Navy authority, operational green.

Direction 10

The Bridge

Navy sidebar · White content · Dark Teal accent · Crimson EBB marker. A deliberate bridge between EBB's navy authority and WizardCD's existing teal identity. The crimson rule on the logo remains as the EBB sub-brand marker, but teal takes over as the product's own accent color. Panel left-borders use crimson (EBB). CTA buttons and active states use dark teal. Both identities coexist without conflict. Brand continuity + product identity.
