WizardCD — Design Directions v3

All 10 directions share one locked foundation: the EBB navy sidebar and the Pure Wordmark logo. What varies is the CD accent — each option explored against the same base so you can compare apples to apples. Pick the CD color that fits the identity you want WizardCD to project.

Locked foundation (all 10 directions):
Sidebar background: #0D2240 — confirmed from EBB logo dark variant & EBB_Systems_Design_System.md
Logo: "WizardCD" in Plus Jakarta Sans 700 — "Wizard" in white, "CD" in the direction's accent color
No icon, no sub-text, no rules. The name is the brand.
N1

Warm gold on navy — the combination of financial authority and premium craftsmanship. Bloomberg, Reuters, gold-standard professional tools all use this pairing. The amber CD stands out clearly against the navy sidebar and anchors the warm-white content area. Confident without being aggressive.

Crimson

#8B1A1A

EBB's exact crimson. This makes WizardCD the clearest possible family member — navy sidebar from EBB's dark logo, crimson accent from EBB's wordmark accent. The content background shifts to parchment to match the warmth of EBB's cream brand. Any client who knows EBB Systems will immediately trust this product. Any new client will perceive the same established gravitas.

The background takes on a faint cool tint to reinforce the technical, precise feeling. Clean without being cold. Precise without being clinical.

This direction will stand out from the sea of blue enterprise dashboards without sacrificing authority.

N5

Ice

#4A90B8

The most restrained of all 10. The CD accent is a steel ice-blue — barely a color, almost a tone. The whole design reads as monochromatic: navy, ice, cool white. No warmth, no drama. This is the direction for a tool so confident it doesn't need to announce itself. Think of the aesthetic of a high-end consultancy's internal tooling — technically flawless, visually neutral.

Copper

#B45309

Darker and more oxidized than amber. Copper reads as aged craftsmanship — the material of precision instruments, artisan tools, quality hardware. Against navy, it creates the palette of old naval officers' kit: authoritative, functional, beautifully made. The warm parchment background reinforces the premium feel. This is the direction that communicates "built to last."

Violet

The most distinctive of the 10. Navy + violet doesn't exist anywhere in the mainstream deployment tool market — which is exactly the point. The background takes a faint warm-violet tint to tie the palette together.

White CD

White

The most opinionated direction: "CD" is white — the same color as "Wizard" — but bold weight while "Wizard" is regular. The differentiation is weight, not color. Zero decoration. The sidebar accent for active items uses navy (the sidebar color reversed on the content side), making the whole design feel like a two-color system. For teams that want absolute visual restraint.

N10

Terracotta

#C2410C

The warmest, earthiest direction. Terracotta on navy is entirely different from amber or copper — it's more organic, more human. The slightly orange tone gives WizardCD a warmth and approachability that pure professional tools often lack, without sacrificing authority. Background shifts to a barely-there warm linen. This is the direction that clients will remember — the one that doesn't look like any other deployment tool.
